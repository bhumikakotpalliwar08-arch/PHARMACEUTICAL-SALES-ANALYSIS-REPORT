# PHARMACEUTICAL-SALES-ANALYSIS-REPORT
 💊 Pharmaceutical Sales Analysis Report

## 📌 Project Overview

The Pharmaceutical Sales Analysis Report is a data analytics project developed using Python to analyze pharmaceutical drug sales. The project explores sales quantities across ATC (Anatomical Therapeutic Chemical) drug categories, identifies top-selling drug categories, and examines monthly sales trends for respiratory drugs.

The analysis uses data visualization and statistical summaries to identify patterns in drug demand and support data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total sales quantity across different ATC drug categories.
- Identify the highest-selling drug category.
- Find the top-selling drugs during selected periods.
- Identify the drug with the highest total sales quantity in 2017.
- Calculate average daily sales for the leading drug category.
- Analyze monthly sales trends for respiratory drugs (R03).
- Visualize sales patterns using charts.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python – Data analysis and calculations.
- Pandas– Data manipulation, aggregation, and summarization.
- Matplotlib– Creating sales charts and visualizations.
- Jupyter Notebook / Python Environment – Running the analysis.

## 📊 Key Analysis and Findings

### 1. Total Sales Quantity by ATC Drug Category

The analysis compares total sales quantities across different ATC drug categories.

Key finding: N02BE recorded the highest total sales quantity, with approximately 63,085 units, followed by N05B and R03.

The bar chart highlights the differences in sales volume across the analyzed categories.

### 2. Top-Selling Drug Categories

The analysis identifies N02BE as the leading category by total sales quantity. Other categories, including N05B, R03, M01AB, and M01AE, also contribute to the overall sales volume.

### 3. Top Drugs During Selected Periods

The report examines the top three drugs in selected periods:

- January 2015
- July 2016
- September 2017

This comparison helps explore how sales rankings vary across different periods.

### 4. Most-Sold Drug in 2017

N02BE was identified as the most-sold drug category in 2017, with approximately 9,258 units.

### 5. Highest Average Daily Sales

N02BE recorded the highest average daily sales, at approximately 29.92 units per day.

### 6. Monthly Sales Trend for Respiratory Drugs (R03)

The monthly trend analysis shows fluctuations in respiratory drug sales throughout the year.

**Key observations:**
- Sales were highest in January, at approximately 1,264 units.
- Sales declined during the first half of the year.
- July recorded the lowest monthly sales, at approximately 550 units.
- Sales recovered during August and October.
- December showed another increase, reaching approximately 1,225 units.

These patterns highlight changes in monthly sales volume and provide a basis for further investigation into demand trends.
